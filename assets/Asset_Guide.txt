# 🎨 Assets & Customization Guide

This directory holds the media assets for the Animus Launcher. To maintain open-source compliance, default copyrighted media files are excluded from this repository.

To customize the default launcher UI with your own background art and audio before compiling, place your files in this folder using the exact filenames listed below:

---

## 🎵 Audio Files
- `LauncherAmbientMusic.mp3` — Background loop played during launcher idle state.


---

## 🖼 Image & Video Files
- `ac3.jpg` — cover art used for the Assassin's Creed 3 sequence panel.
- `ac4.jpg` — cover art used for the Assassin's Creed 4: Black Flag sequence panel.
- `acfreedomcry.jpg` — cover art used for the Assassin's Creed Freedom Cry sequence panel.
- `acliberation.jpg` — cover art used for the Assassin's Creed Liberation sequence panel.


---

> **Note:** If no custom media is supplied, the launcher will automatically fall back to the silent/minimal UI layout.
